## 💡 Why This Matters

The Singleton pattern ensures:

* Only **one instance** of a class exists
* A **global access point** to that instance
* Better control over shared resources like logging, configuration, or database connections

Understanding this pattern is a key step toward mastering **object-oriented design** and preparing for more advanced patterns like Factory and Builder.
